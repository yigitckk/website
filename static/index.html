02Method

  1. A tool's exit code verifies its own job, not my claim. "Install succeeded" doesn't mean the phone runs my code. Every claim of done gets its own check — timestamps compared, screens actually looked at.
  2. Freeze, don't delete. When a direction dies, its code moves to parked/ with written return-conditions. Deleting feels irreversible, so the decision gets postponed — freezing makes the decision cheap enough to take today.
  3. Every plan states what would kill it. A growth stage without a death condition is not a plan, it's a wish. Thresholds are written before the test, not after the result.
  4. Lessons need a control group. "The winner did X" means nothing until you check whether the dead did X too. Most of them did.
  5. Old documents don't get corrected — they get dated. A wrong answer, kept next to when and why it was falsified, is a record. Overwritten, it's just today's opinion.
